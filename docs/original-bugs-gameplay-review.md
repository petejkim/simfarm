## Conditional high-severity arithmetic cases

These can be harmful in the demonstrated boundary state, but should not displace ordinary-play issues merely because the hypothetical outcome is severe.

- **SF-044 + SF-067 (pre-correction assessment):** a zero tax assessment was treated as unpaid; subsequent zero-amount default could sell assets and request restart/exit. Both native paths were retained at review time. SF-044 is now corrected as noted above. The ledger's zero-land/zero-sales fixture does not prove this combination arises on a normal original farm with its protected homestead; ordinary original reachability remains unproven.
- **SF-003 + SF-019:** signed field-age wrapping/negative effective age can give wrong crop graphics or a fully mature valuation. Normal harvest resets age and annual crops expire earlier. Investigate long-lived fixed-harvest crops after Schedule CUT/removal of future harvests; the earlier notes do not constitute a completed live reproduction.
- **SF-020:** low-word harvest valuation can slash proceeds after quantity×unit-price exceeds65535. A raw shipped-definition check for all24 crops, at twice their base price, found **no overflow**; maximum product is39140 (oranges: quantity103, base1904). This narrows priority under ordinary weekly price bounds. It does not cover altered SSM prices, every carried contract, unusual inventory quantities or all other price producers.
- **SF-055:** an unsigned sale quote≥32768 is sign-extended into negative cash proceeds. Supplied equipment prices avoid it; demonstrate normal animal bonus/quote growth before changing sale arithmetic. Building book-value subtraction has a related signed-price boundary.
- **SF-043 / SF-045:** signed sales tax after2^31 turnover and word-truncated town maintenance above327 residential levels can produce surprising bills/accounting. The arithmetic is verified; normal reachable turnover/town constraints need stronger evidence.
- **SF-014 / SF-065:** original-file accounting transfers can omit/overwrite a ledger or retain previous-session values. Current native checkpoints persist their own accounts. Cold imports, warm SSM imports and new-game setup must be evaluated separately; early ledger descriptions of missing integration are stale.

No browser/build rerun was required for this documentation-only review.

The shipped-price bound above is reproducible from the24 `.CRP` files: read quantity at `0x1500+32`, base price at `0x1500+34`, then calculate `quantity * floor(2*basePrice/10)`. It is an arithmetic bound under the stated assumptions, not a full trading-session simulation.

# Original finances

Evidence refers to supplied `SIMFARM.EXE`, SHA-256 `8b86a2d3d07e0b6f77ce491e27e538feeccf5d1af149f42bc0f6a0631c3e58ce`. Addresses are file offsets. Analysis is static; no DOS code is executed. The provisional monthly `$40 + debt*0.005` charge in `../simfarm2/src/simulation.js` is not original-game evidence and is not used here.

## Tax assessment — implemented primitive, high confidence

Resident `1a66:0cc0`, file `0x1d520..0x1d5c2`, calculates annual payment. Its cash comparisons are unsigned; division helper `0084:33f8` / `0x5e38` is signed long division, truncating toward zero.

| Cash (`6190:3e48`) | Divisor applied to sales (`6190:3e44`) |
| --- | --- |
| Below 150,000 | 10 |
| 150,000 through 499,999 | 5 |
| 500,000 and above | 3 |

The dword result is `trunc(signed32(sales)/divisor) + unpaidTaxes + trunc(playerLandValue/99)`, with 32-bit addition wrapping. It uses the sales accumulator, not crop-income ledger `3e60` or net profit. SF-026's stored-sale double posting therefore does not itself double this sales component. Unpaid taxes are `6190:7518`, restored from SSM `0x21726` by `0x4622e..0x46243`. Countdown byte `4d9f:fed7` follows at SSM `0x2172a`. The calculator reads debt regardless of countdown. Debug strings at `0x8e3ad`, `0x8e3e1` and `0x8e408` corroborate the tax interpretation.

Land value comes through manager `24e6:10c8`, thunk file `0x28128`, to overlay 15:2008 / `0x3cba8..0x3cc1f`. It scans the 12×12 table, summing only ownership code **1** through existing valuation `0x3c766`. Other ownership codes do not contribute plots, but all nonzero codes increase the common per-plot base. Each quote clears DX at `0x3c83b`; summation uses ADD/ADC. Fertility, current signed soil moisture and first-map-word bit `0x1000` affect taxation as they affect buying land.

`src/simulation/tax.ts` ports valuation and assessment. Arrears are explicit input until payment state is integrated. This primitive neither resets sales nor charges cash. Tests pin source instructions, both thresholds, signed/unsigned boundaries, separate truncation, ownership codes, map corners, all supplied farms and non-mutation. SF-043 records signed-sales overflow without claiming ordinary-play reachability.

## Calendar and payment dependencies — traced, not integrated

Monthly `0xcc40..0xccee` retries a balance at `2d9f:d5ba` while countdown `4d9f:fed6` is nonzero, through debit category 1000. Tax retries `0xccee..0xcd9a` use countdown `fed7`, debt `6190:7518` and category 2500. Failed attempts decrement the countdown and eventually call forced-sale routine `1a66:0f06` / `0x1d766` with reason 39 or 36. The full default/forced-sale behavior and first balance's origination remain to be recovered; no flat monthly upkeep or interest rate is established.

Months 6 and 9 (zero-based, after increment) warn when assessment exceeds cash with no active tax countdown (`0xcd9a..0xcddd`). At year-end, after old-plan row 48 executes and month resets, `0xcdf4` calls annual collection `0x1d5c4`, `0xcdf9` calls ledger reset `0x1d396`, then `0xcdfe` projects crop plans. The native clock currently implements only those schedule operations.

Annual collection calculates tax, clears sales first, attempts debit category 2500, then recomputes total town residential level through manager `10dc` → overlay 15:067a / `0x3b21a`. This scans town-owned regions (code 2) through counter `0x3c5c8` (the high nibble of the first regional byte, not a population count). Failed payment adjusts existing debt, increments `6190:3eed`, writes negative assessment to `6190:758a`, emits notice 159 and sets the countdown to three (`0x1d615..0x1d67e`). The debt formula includes both old debt and the assessment, which already includes old debt; retain that observation pending the complete lifecycle before judging intent.

Successful payment allocates receipts between town balances `758a/758e` using a low-word `residentialLevels*200` threshold and signed dword comparisons (`0x1d684..0x1d765`). Some branches invoke town development through manager `10d2` → overlay 15:13da / `0x3bf7a`. These balances/callbacks need reconstruction and checkpointing before connecting annual collection. A standalone tax debit would omit original town behavior.

## Annual settlement and town expansion reservation — implemented primitives

`settleAnnualTax` now implements the financial branches of `0x1d5c4..0x1d765`. Its explicit account stores raw dword bits for unpaid taxes, tax expenses, town revenue and expansion funds, plus byte failure/countdown fields. It validates all account inputs before mutation, clears sales before attempted payment, and posts successful payment to the tax ledger. This account is not yet part of live GameSimulation/checkpoints; no saved field meanings are guessed.

Failure computes `oldDebt + (oldDebt >>> 2) + (signed32(assessment) >> 2) + assessment`, retaining 32 bits; increments the failure byte with wrapping; sets revenue to negative assessment and countdown to three. A zero assessment takes this branch too (SF-044). Success intentionally leaves debt/countdown unchanged, matching the original; the implications across the monthly retry lifecycle remain to be tested. Tax-ledger addition wraps as before.

Town maintenance is the low word of `totalResidentialLevels * 200` (SF-045). Let R be revenue after adding the payment, F be expansion funds, and M maintenance. If signed R < M, combined signed `(R+F)` below M leaves revenue `R+F-M` and zero funds; otherwise the reserve covers the shortfall, leaving zero revenue and funds `R+F-M`. Neither branch offers expansion. Otherwise a signed arithmetic quarter of the **payment**, not total revenue, moves to expansion funds; revenue decreases by that quarter plus maintenance, then the town-expansion routine is requested. The return flag is an explicit dependency, not a constructed district.

`townStatistics` ports `0x3b21a` and `0x3c5c8`. Loader `0x45a96..0x45abf` proves regional channel 0 maps to `6190:949c`: low nibble is district type and high nibble its level. Count/sum includes only ownership 2. Debug text at DS `9d50` calls the sum “Total Residential Level”; its displayed maintenance multiplier 8 differs from actual annual allocation’s 200, so the display string must not supply the simulation rule.

`findTownExpansionSite` ports `0x3c3e6..0x3c4f7`: radii 1–11 around saved town origin, scanning each inclusive square x-first, y-second. It skips coordinates outside 0–11, nonzero ownership and any plot tile with **terrain** bits `0x3000`. It does not impose adjacency, use Euclidean distance or check overlay flags. `beginTownExpansion` ports the pre-choice operations of `0x3bf7a`: if signed expansion funds afford 15,000 and a site exists, deduct that amount and set ownership 2, returning a pending site without changing player cash or map tiles. The original town quote `0x3c840` averages two locals that remain zero, so only base `6190:7516` contributes.

Inspected imported original `public/assets/twnchoic.bmp.png` (archive member `twnchoic.bmp`, generated asset remains ignored). The four choices are Residential (types 6–13), Commercial (types 1–3), Airport (type 5) and Recreational (type 4). They call district constructor `0x3aba0`. Timeout `0x3c37f..0x3c3a3` selects `below(14)+1`; interactive variants consume the original simulation RNG differently. Choice resolution, constructor/road connections, recreation restrictions, airport effects and the town growth/degradation tick remain pending. The browser implementation must retain a pending choice in simulation state and native checkpoints, with React sending the selection.

## Livestock and equipment sales — implemented primitives

`asset-sale.ts` now ports the livestock and equipment branches of generic sale `0x4c41c..0x4c60e` (manager `24e6:0fe2` → overlay 27:0fcc). These functions operate on the plain simulation world and explicit `AssetSaleState`; they do not yet add shop controls, live default handling or new native checkpoint fields. Stored-crop sales remain in `harvest-sale.ts` with their distinct unsigned-price/double-income behavior.

### Additional saved records

Loader `0x458e0..0x45909` reads five 18-byte animal definitions from SSM `0x20cbe..0x20d17` into `6190:70e2`. The first four types are 160–163; the fifth definition repeats type 163 but has a different price. Its use is not inferred or normalized. All five complete records are copied, retaining unknown bytes and pointers. Existing goods-definition layout applies: count word +4, price word +6, width/height bytes +8/+9. Native `readAnimalDefinitions` preserves these separately until finance/save integration.

Sequential dword reads establish equipment book value SSM `0x2167c` → `6190:3e50` (`0x45c76`), equipment income `0x21694` → `3e64` (`0x45d06`), and livestock income `0x216a0` → `3e74` (`0x45d4e`). `readAssetSaleState` detaches those values and the definitions. Source bytes remain unchanged. This does not claim the broader balance-sheet interpretation is fully reconstructed.

### Quotes and accounting

Equipment quote `0x1192e..0x11970` returns unsigned `definition.price >> (record.byte17 >> 6)`, and display condition band `3-(byte17>>6)`. Increasing that byte reduces the price. Livestock quote `0x12958..0x12a4d` instead derives band `record.byte8 >> 6`, adds a **word-truncated** bonus from record word +10 (multipliers 3/5/3/2 for types 160/161/162/163; otherwise zero), then logically shifts the dword sum right by `3-band`. The quote's returned AX is retained as a word. The price lookup adds 96 to the type's low byte, wrapping before indexing `70e8`; native access accepts only the five loaded definitions (types 160–164), rejecting unknown types instead of indexing neighboring memory. Record +10 remains named `valuationWord`; no weight/age semantics are assumed from this arithmetic alone.

Both sale callers sign-extend AX (`0x4c502`, `0x4c5a5`), unlike crop sales, so a quote >=32768 becomes a negative amount (SF-055). Cash adds those raw dword bits then applies the original unsigned 9,999,999 cap. The sales accumulator and the appropriate income ledger add the same amount modulo 2^32. Equipment category 0 and animal category 160 do not add that income in the credit dispatcher; the sale caller posts it once. A zero-value sale still removes the asset.

### Removal, eligibility and native guards

Equipment requires active bit `0x20` and neither reserved bit 1 nor field-work bit 8 (`0x4c4d4..0x4c4f0`). Moving bit 2 alone does not reject a sale. The generic sale does not reject rented bit `0x40`; the forced-sale selector does. Native primitives preserve this distinction (SF-058), while prevalidating a nonzero definition count because the original removal refuses zero count after payment (extension of SF-027).

Generic map removal `0x1d0e8` first checks for a building at the asset position. Matching storage cells are cleared and the existing interior renderer toggled twice to retain the open/closed state. Ground equipment uses its direction-adjusted dimensions (`0x10d9c`), livestock uses definition dimensions. Outside storage, these two categories only clear terrain `0x0800`, leaving overlay words and other terrain flags intact. Aircraft type 8 instead restores moisture/attribute-based grass across its definition footprint. Native validation checks footprints and stored cross-references before payment and rejects live attachment references that would become dangling (SF-057).

Record deletion `0x3a040` decrements an owned equipment definition count and subtracts its **full definition price**, not depreciated proceeds, from book value. Rentals do neither. Animal definition count decreases once. Record deleters `0xf888` and `0x11b4c` clear only active bit; unknown bytes and other flags remain. The native active count is derived from bounded records. Stored-animal deletion's second storage-clear call has no additional effect after the generic removal; native validation requires the saved building reference to match.

Aircraft deletion also calls `0x319ee..0x31acb`: home bytes +50/+51 locate a 2×2 patch reset to tile 23 while retaining terrain upper bits. Home X modulo 8 equal to 3 clears regional channel-0 bit `0x20`; equal to 5 clears bit `0x40`; other positions do not change the region byte. These airport occupancy bits overlap the byte interpreted by town routines as level/type, so that high nibble must not be globally treated as independent of aircraft. Native code bounds the complete home patch before payment. Aircraft flight/rental behavior is still not reconstructed.

Seven tests pin loader/quote/sale/removal instructions, all eight saved tables, all 256 condition bytes, truncated multiplication and signed proceeds/cash wrap, every active original animal and machine, storage and unknown-byte preservation, busy/rental rules, aircraft cleanup and atomic invalid-state rejection. These are complete sale primitives for the supported source records, not completed default or livestock simulation.

The source count-controlled default loops can overrun records after successful removals when debt remains (SF-059); native traversal must be bounded rather than reproducing an out-of-array scan.

## Field teardown and clearing effects — implemented prerequisites

Default calls field cleanup `1957:0226` / `0x1b996` at `0x1da1d`, then record deactivation `1957:02fa` / `0x1ba6a` at `0x1da28`. Native `removeField` composes those calls. For nonzero field state, cleanup erases and releases both machine assignment words +42/+44, in order. Waiting state zero skips this entirely. Release resets reserved/work flags, dimensions and machine field byte +48; rented machines deactivate. Rented aircraft additionally run home cleanup `0x319ee`, now shared with equipment sale. This does not implement aircraft allocation or flight.

Cleanup visits the saved field rectangle x-first, enqueues each tile for clearing, and clears overlay bit `0x1000` (`0x1ba47`). Deactivation clears only field byte +7 bit 1; assignments, crop state, unknown bytes and all plan words remain in the inactive record. Native active counts derive from records. Source cleanup does not detach released chains; native cleanup repairs those known links to avoid references to inactive rentals (SF-063), preserving truck +44 because it denotes storage.

The rectangle selector `0x1b868` tests four query corners against hardcoded 8×8 field bounds. Its exclusive far corners can select an adjacent field and it misses an enclosed field. Native `findOverlappingField` uses strict positive-area overlap and actual saved dimensions (SF-062). This correction is explicit; it is not a literal translation of that defective query.

Clearing enqueue `0636:1d96` / `0xa2f6..0xa416` uses 64 x/y byte entries at `2d9f:d53a/d57a`, cursor `6190:1eaa`, count `1ea7`, and last-animation tick `1ea8`. Cursor increments modulo 64 before use; count increments capped at 64 even when replacing an occupied slot. Replaced coordinates restore tile 55 if terrain bits `0x3000` are set, otherwise `15 + signed moisture + (attribute >>> 2)`, retaining upper terrain bits and word arithmetic. New coordinates receive tile 664 via exact mask `(terrain & 0xfa98) | 0x298`. Overlay/attributes are untouched by the queue itself.

Update `0xa418..0xa5ba`, called from world dispatcher `0xbcc8`, waits for unsigned 16-bit elapsed ticks >=16. It advances eligible images by two (664→666→668→670→672), then restores terrain and marks x=255. Source's special tree branch for intermediate value 670 is retained although ordinary enqueue's even sequence does not enter it. The loop compares visited entries against the **changing** count using `>`; a full queue retires 33 entries on its first finishing update, leaving 31. Native code retains this work budget but bounds the slot scan at 64 (SF-061). Coordinates/counters/moisture validate before mutation.

The initialized 128 coordinate bytes at EXE `0x5dd1a..0x5dd99` are zero, although enqueue's free sentinel is 255. No explicit array reset has yet been found; general startup initialization remains untraced. Native fresh queues initialize x to 255 to avoid phantom eviction at (0,0), without claiming a proven original startup defect (SF-060). The queue is explicit runtime state, not yet live animation/checkpoint state. Eight tests cover source instructions, ring eviction, wrapped timing, changing-count retirement, all original fields in eight farms, machinery/rentals, corrected overlap and atomic malformed-input rejection.

Land-sale trace `0x3c9c2..0x3cba6` confirms ownership 1 is required, valuation precedes mutation, category 2000 credits cash, optional caller flag controls sales accumulation, and land income always increases. Default passes flag zero. It clears ownership, removes buildings encountered across the 8×8 plot, clears overlay `0x4800`, preserves terrain tiles 39–68 and restores grass elsewhere, then repairs neighboring fences via `0x3d6da`. It does not itself remove fields: default does that first. Building demolition `0x19f6a` removes each nonempty stored content item through generic deletion, restores/clears occupancy on the footprint and queues clearing effects; the caller then deletes the building and subtracts its full definition price from book value. Those building/content and neighbor-fence dependencies remain to be implemented before land sale/default can be enabled.

## Land sale — implemented primitive

`sellLand` now composes `0x3c9c2..0x3cba6` with the recovered building/content demolition and neighboring fence repair. It accepts explicit land income, demolition/asset state and clearing runtime. Original land income is loaded from SSM `0x21698` into `6190:3e68` by the dword read at `0x45d1e..0x45d33`. Full live financial state/checkpoint integration remains pending; the building ledger still has the unresolved lifecycle described above.

The operation requires ownership 1 and takes its quote before changing ownership or terrain. Cash adds the unsigned quote with dword wrap and the original 9,999,999 cap. Credit category 2000 has no expense-reversal branch in `0x1ca28..0x1cb41`, so land expenses remain unchanged. A nonzero caller flag adds the quote to the sales accumulator; forced-sale/default passes zero. Land income always receives one wrapped addition. Ownership changes to zero before map processing.

For each tile x-first/y-second, the first remaining active overlapping building is demolished in full—even if its footprint extends beyond the plot. The tile's overlay loses `0x4800`; only terrain images 39–68 survive unchanged. Other terrain receives grass based on signed moisture and the attribute's upper six bits, retaining terrain high bits and word arithmetic. Neighboring fences are repaired last. Notices are represented by the ordered `demolishedBuildings` result; DOS viewport refresh has no native equivalent. Fields and plans are untouched by this operation: default must call the separate overlap/removal sequence first. No implicit home protection is inserted; the original default selector protects its home plot before calling this function.

A synchronous demolition preparation phase validates all encountered buildings before cash, ownership or world mutations. It shares counts across the transaction, so two individually valid buildings with an insufficient combined definition count cannot cause a partial sale. Equipment references between machines that will all be destroyed are allowed; references from surviving machines reject. Prepared callbacks then execute at the original per-tile encounter points, preserving map/animation ordering. This extends SF-064's native atomicity correction to the full transaction.
